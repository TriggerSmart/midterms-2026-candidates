Data access

METHODOLOGY

What the dashboard means

Candidate roster

The primary nationwide race layer is a structured 2026 House, Senate and governor roster, with a separate minor-party/independent file merged into each race. Federal identity and finance are cross-checked against the Federal Election Commission when a profile is opened. State ballot authorities remain the final authority.

Forecasts

The projection layer displays a third-party race model for nationwide coverage and overlays published categorical rating consensus where those tables can be matched. Neither is an official result or this site's own prediction.

Issue positions

Issue panels show source sentences mentioning a policy topic. They do not infer a position when the source text does not state one. Missing data is shown as missing.

Public-record risk

The score only counts operator-verified public legal, ethics, regulatory or compliance evidence. Ideology, party, protected traits, family, health, rumor and ordinary controversy are never scored.
